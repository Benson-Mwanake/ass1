About Our Department

The Department of Information Technology focuses on helping students develop practical skills in computing and modern technology. Students are introduced to areas such as programming, web development, databases, networking and computer systems.

The department provides students with knowledge that can help them solve real-world problems using information technology.

IT Laboratory

Our computer laboratory provides a suitable environment for students to practice their technical and programming skills.
